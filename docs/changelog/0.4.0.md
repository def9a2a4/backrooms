# v0.4.0 - Eight new levels: The Henge, the scan levels and more

> **Upgrading from 0.3.1:** just replace the jar. Your config, levels and player progress carry over.
> The new levels are created the first time the server starts with this version. The levels built
> from 3D meshes (Stonehenge, the test scene and the four scans, ~32 MB in total) download them into
> `plugins/Backrooms/meshes/` on that first start. To skip the download, set
> `meshes.download: false` in config.yml before starting; those levels are then turned off.

- **New level: The Henge (Level 56).** Stonehenge at sixteen times its real size: a ring of stones
  about 530 blocks across and up to 100 blocks tall, standing on a round floating island in grey
  fog at the midsummer dawn. The stones come from a real 3D scan, so every sarsen and lintel is
  where it is at the real monument, with moss and lichen where the scan has them. Walk off the edge
  of the island and the fog puts you back where you started.

- **Giant pigs.** Each time someone arrives, a giant pig is born inside the circle, up to eight at a
  time. Killing one gets you a single porkchop, and a reminder of where meat used to come from.

- **The altar.** At the center of the circle stands a small polished diorite pyramid with a
  lodestone on top. Drop a porkchop on the lodestone and you're taken to Hintze Hall, or to The
  Server Room if the scan levels are off. Any porkchop works.

- **Getting there.** The Server Room has a new kind of command block for it. Its lectern hints talk
  about the solstice, the stones and the herds people used to keep.

- **Small download.** The whole monument ships as a 50 KB file inside the jar; the blocks are
  worked out as the world generates.

- **Four more levels built from real 3D scans, at sixteen times life size.** Like The Henge, these
  places are voxelized from photogrammetry as the worlds generate, now with a block chosen for
  every block-sized patch of the scan's surface, from every kind of stone, terracotta and
  concrete in the game.

- **New level: Hintze Hall (Level 1881).** The Natural History Museum's main hall in London, after
  hours, with the lights on: terracotta arches, the grand staircase, glass roof lights, the blue
  whale skeleton hanging over the nave. Placards on lecterns stand around the entrance, about
  Stonehenge, Göbekli Tepe, the museum and Hope the whale. Read one about a place to its last page
  and you're taken there. Reached from a new kind of Server Room command block, and from The
  Henge's altar.

- **New level: The Collections (Level 1882).** Hintze Hall's museum without end: two floors of
  galleries joined by tall arches, laid out as a maze, each district built in its own style
  (terracotta, marble, brick, Gothic, mud brick, prismarine, a library, copper and more). Every
  gallery has a subject, from minerals and fossils to the Nether, the End and fine art: display
  cases, skeletons of real animals and scaled-up mobs, monoliths, pots with sherds, aquariums,
  paintings, plants and trees. Every exhibit has a catalogue label, and lecterns hold catalogues,
  gallery guides and visitors' books. Some rooms are Oxford-style reading rooms with galleries,
  bookcase stalls and learned treatises; dead ends behind a STAFF ONLY door are storerooms full of
  racks, crates, chests of odds and ends, and the museum's own day books, registers and memoranda. Every few hundred blocks a vaulted central
  hall opens up, with a balcony, a grand staircase, rose windows and a whale, dragon, sauropod,
  theropod or mammoth. Not connected to other levels yet.

- **New level: Göbekli Tepe (Level 9500).** The 11,500-year-old stone enclosures in Anatolia, older
  than writing and older than farming, on a floating island in warm haze. Shake seeds out of the
  wild grass, grow wheat by the pool, bake bread and lay it on the offering stone. Crops grow fast
  here, and the seeds, the wheat and the bread all have something to say about where they lead.
  Reached from the museum.

- **New level: The Undercroft (Level 1967).** The concrete space under London's Southbank Centre,
  in greys and pitch dark, with a few candles burning. Put down any light, and someone notices.

- **New level: The Undercroft, Later (Level 1973).** The same place after the skaters, BMXers and
  graffiti writers made it theirs: painted, lit and loud. Skate around it long enough and it lets
  you go.

- **Downloads, not a bigger jar.** The scans behind these four levels are 7-9 MB each, so they're
  fetched once from this project's GitHub Pages site and checked against checksums built into the
  plugin. Stonehenge and the test scene's models are downloaded the same way, keeping the jar
  small. The
  server logs the download size and the free disk space before starting, and warns that the scan
  worlds grow to several hundred MB each as they're explored. The levels open as soon as their mesh
  is ready; an exit into one that isn't ready yet tells you to try again soon.

- **New level: The Sponge (Level 590).** Menger sponges built from sponge blocks, each 243 blocks on
  a side, laid edge to edge forever in a single layer and hanging in yellow-grey fog. You start
  deep inside, in a narrow 9-block side tunnel, and have to find your way out to the big open
  spaces. Holes at every size, from one block wide up to 81, run straight through. Fall out the
  bottom and you're put back where you started. Reached from its own Server Room command block;
  somewhere in all that dry sponge, a block is wet.

- **New level: Planetoids (Level 974).** Small round worlds hanging in black space, like the old
  Planetoids world type, in clusters separated by long empty stretches. There are about sixty
  kinds, most a shell around a core: leaves around every kind of wood, stone or deepslate around
  ores, grass and dirt around water, hollow amethyst geodes, sculk around unbreakable deepslate,
  nether, soul sand, basalt and end worlds, honey, pumpkins, bookshelves, glass marbles, banded gas
  giants, glowing stars, and a very rare one with ancient debris at its heart. Here and there a few
  smaller, lumpier asteroids drift between them, full of good ores. Fall into the void and you're
  put back on the planet you started on. Reached from a book in The Library; the grass planets
  are hiding something at their cores.

- **New level: I'm a Teapot (Level 418).** You've fallen out of Minecraft's block engine and into a
  voxel engine's test scene, and the server greets you with "418 I'm a teapot". An endless white and
  grey checkerboard stretches into the fog, scattered with the classic computer graphics test
  models: Utah teapots (lots of them), Suzanne the monkey, the Stanford bunny, dragon and Lucy, plus
  cubes, pyramids, cones, spheres, tori and Cornell boxes. Everything is flat colored concrete,
  upright or tumbled at any angle, and nothing quite touches the floor: things hover a block or
  more above it, or float high up. The further you walk from where you arrive, the bigger things
  get, up to two hundred blocks tall. The dragon and Lucy only come large, where there are enough
  blocks to show them off. Teapots are hollow.

- **Getting back to block land.** Somewhere in the scene there is exactly one real piece of
  Minecraft: a little stone cube with dirt on top and a single oak log, sitting on the floor as if
  it still obeys gravity. Break wood and the block engine comes back... almost. You land on the
  Skyblock island. There's one of these cubes in every 384 by 384 block area.

- **Getting there.** The Server Room has another new kind of command block for it, with lecterns
  about teapots, coffee and RFC 2324. From the overworld you can brew tea: put a water cauldron over
  a lit campfire (or a fire) and drop some leaves next to it.

- **New advancements** for finding each new level, for brewing tea, and for breaking the wood in
  Level 418.

- **New config option `meshes`.** `download: false` turns the levels built from meshes off (56, 418
  and the scan levels; a mesh you put in `meshes/` yourself still loads); `base_url` points the
  downloads at a mirror.

- **For level authors:**
  - Exit types: `item_offering` sends a player on when an item they dropped comes to rest on a
    chosen block; `break_block` and `place_block` when they break or place one of the listed
    blocks (material names, or tags such as `#minecraft:logs`); `lectern_last_page` when they turn
    a lectern's book to its last page.
  - `fallback_level` on any exit, used while its target is unavailable.
  - `enter_title` / `enter_subtitle` show a title when a player arrives in a level, and
    `random_tick_speed` sets a level's random tick speed.
  - A generic `scan` generator (`mode: shell` for interiors, `column` for terrain) with floor props
    (display cases, an offering pedestal, farm plots, wild grass, single blocks, lecterns holding
    placard books) that rebuild themselves a few seconds after being broken.
  - The Henge's pig size, health, the cap on pigs, and the porkchop's name and lore are set under
    `giant_pig:` in `levels/level_56.yml`.
