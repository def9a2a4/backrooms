# v0.5.0 - The scan levels: Hintze Hall, The Undercroft, Göbekli Tepe

> **Upgrading from 0.4.0:** just replace the jar. Your config, levels and player progress carry over.
> On the first start, the server downloads the four new levels' meshes (~32 MB in total) into
> `plugins/Backrooms/meshes/`. To skip that, set `scan_levels.download: false` in config.yml
> before starting; the new levels are then turned off.

- **Four new levels built from real 3D scans, at sixteen times life size.** Like The Henge, the
  places are voxelized from photogrammetry as the worlds generate, now with a block chosen for
  every block-sized patch of the scan's surface, from every kind of stone, terracotta and
  concrete in the game.

- **New level: Hintze Hall (Level 1881).** The Natural History Museum's main hall in London, after
  hours, with the lights on: terracotta arches, the grand staircase, the blue whale skeleton
  hanging over the nave. Glass display cases stand on the floor. Break an exhibit out of its case
  to go where it came from. Reached from a new kind of Server Room command block.

- **New level: Göbekli Tepe (Level 9500).** The 11,500-year-old stone enclosures in Anatolia, older
  than writing and older than farming, on a floating island in warm haze. Shake seeds out of the
  wild grass, grow wheat by the pool, bake bread and lay it on the offering stone. Crops grow fast
  here, and the seeds, the wheat and the bread all have something to say about where they lead.
  Reached from the museum.

- **New level: The Undercroft (Level 1967).** The concrete space under London's Southbank Centre,
  in greys and pitch dark, with a few candles burning. Put down any light, and someone notices.

- **New level: The Undercroft, Later (Level 1973).** The same place after the skaters, BMXers and
  graffiti writers made it theirs: painted, lit and loud. Skate around it long enough and it lets
  you go.

- **The Henge now leads to the museum.** The porkchop offering takes you to Hintze Hall, or to The
  Server Room if the scan levels are off.

- **Downloads, not a bigger jar.** The scans are 7-9 MB each, so they're fetched once from
  def9a2a4.github.io and checked against checksums built into the plugin. The server logs the
  download size and the free disk space before starting, and warns that the scan worlds grow to
  several hundred MB each as they're explored. The levels open as soon as their mesh is ready; an
  exit into one that isn't ready yet tells you to try again soon.

- **New config option `scan_levels`.** `download: false` turns the scan levels off (a mesh you put in
  `meshes/` yourself still loads); `base_url` points the downloads at a mirror.

- **For level authors:** a generic `scan` generator (`mode: shell` for interiors, `column` for
  terrain) with floor props (display cases, an offering pedestal, farm plots, wild grass, single
  blocks), a `place_block` exit trigger, `fallback_level` on any exit (used while its target is
  unavailable), and a `random_tick_speed` level option.
