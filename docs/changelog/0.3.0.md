# v0.3.0 - Minecraft 26.3, 1.21.11 minimum

> **Upgrading from 0.2.1:** just replace the jar. Your config, levels and player progress carry over.
> The plugin now needs **Paper or Purpur 1.21.11 or newer** (Java 21), or **26.1+** (Java 25).

- **Minecraft 26.3 support.** Tested on Paper and Purpur 26.3.

- **Older servers are refused cleanly.** The bundled datapack and some of the APIs the plugin uses
  only exist from 1.21.11. On anything older, earlier versions either stopped the server from
  booting ("Failed to load datapacks") or crashed with `NoClassDefFoundError: org/bukkit/GameRules`.
  Now the plugin disables itself with a clear message and the server starts normally. Earlier
  versions were listed as compatible with 1.21–1.21.10 by mistake. They never worked there, and no
  backport is planned.

- **Time of day on 26.x.** Levels with a fixed time never had it applied on 26.x. They now do:
  The Hedge Maze sits just after sunset, Alpha at dusk, and The Poolrooms and Skyblock at noon. This
  makes The Hedge Maze and Alpha noticeably darker on 26.x than on 1.21.11, where these levels have
  no time-of-day lighting.

- **Faster startup.** Removed a ~9 second stall at startup, and the "Initializing Legacy Material
  Support… this is a bug!" warning, both caused by The Corrupted Chunk.

- **README updates.** Added the supported versions, and Level 101 (Alpha) with its entrances from
  The Library and The Server Room.

- **Tested on Minecraft 26.x.** Every change is checked on Paper and Purpur 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2
  and 26.3.
